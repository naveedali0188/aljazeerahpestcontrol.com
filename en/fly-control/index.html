Fly Control

Al Jazeerah Pest Control LLC provides Fly Control in the UAE. Book a site inspection and a technician will assess your property and explain the recommended treatment.

  1. InspectionA technician checks the property and identifies the problem.
  2. Treatment planYou receive a plan suited to the pest and the property.
  3. Follow-up adviceYou get guidance on prevention and what to do after treatment.

Treatment steps, product choice and re-entry times depend on the pest, the property, the product label and the technician's instructions.
