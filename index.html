FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is pest control worth it?

For most homes and businesses, yes. Professional treatment handles the infestation at the source, and it usually costs less than the damage pests such as termites can cause.

How often should I treat my house?

Most homes in the UAE are treated every 2 to 3 months. The right schedule depends on the pest, how severe the problem is, and the type of property.

What are the most common pests in the UAE?

Cockroaches, ants, termites, rodents and bed bugs are the most common. We also treat mosquitoes, bees, wasps, lizards and snakes.

What are the signs of a pest infestation?

Seeing pests, hearing noises at night, finding droppings or nests, property damage, and unexplained bites or allergic reactions.

When is the best time of year for pest control?

Spring and autumn are the most effective seasons in the UAE. If you see pests, treat them as soon as you notice them, whatever the time of year.

Which areas do you serve?
